Runtime: all eleven sheets expose nine motion rows and sixteen look directions; Siren, Anoko and Dekatsuyo are currently hidden. Only Chiikawa/Hachiware and Chiikawa/Hachiware/Usagi have supplied shared animations. Petting reuses the wave motion with a drawn heart. Pets can be dragged and show scheduled data bubbles. No feeding, shop or conversational chat UI is enabled.
